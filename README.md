# AI Employee Onboarding Automation

An AI-powered employee onboarding automation workflow built with **n8n, Google Gemini, JavaScript, Google Sheets, and Gmail**.

This workflow automates key parts of the employee onboarding process by collecting employee information, checking for missing onboarding data, identifying required equipment, generating personalized emails, storing onboarding records, and notifying both the employee and HR.

---

## Overview

Employee onboarding often involves collecting information, checking whether required details are available, preparing equipment, updating HR records, and sending multiple emails.

This project automates these repetitive tasks using an n8n workflow powered by Google Gemini.

The workflow receives employee information through a webhook and processes it using JavaScript and an AI Employee Onboarding Assistant.

The AI analyzes the employee information, identifies missing onboarding data, determines equipment requirements, and generates:

- A welcome email for the employee
- An onboarding email for HR

The processed information is then stored in Google Sheets and the generated emails are sent automatically.

How It Works
1. Employee Data Submission

The workflow starts with an HTTP POST request to the n8n Webhook.

The submitted form data contains employee information such as:

Full Name
Email
Phone
Experience
Position
Joining Date
Manager
Department
Job Title
Employment Type
Equipment Required
2. Data Extraction

The JavaScript node extracts the required fields from the incoming webhook payload.

It also converts selected form values into readable text, such as department, employment type, and equipment requirements.

The extracted information is passed to the AI Agent for further processing.

3. AI Employee Onboarding Assistant

Google Gemini processes the employee information through an AI Agent.

The AI onboarding assistant is instructed to:

Check employee information
Identify missing onboarding data
Review required equipment
Generate a welcome email for the employee
Generate an onboarding email for HR

The workflow uses a structured output format to keep the AI response organized.

4. Structured Output

The AI response contains structured fields including:

name
email
phone
experience
position
joining date
manager
department
job title
employment type
equipments required
missing onboarding data
email to employee subject
email to employee
email to HR subject
email to HR

This makes the AI-generated information easier to process in the remaining workflow.

5. Google Sheets Storage

After AI processing, the onboarding information is saved to Google Sheets.

This provides a centralized record of employee onboarding information and AI-generated onboarding communications.

6. Employee Email

The workflow automatically sends the generated welcome/onboarding email to the employee's email address.

The email subject and message are generated by the AI Agent.

7. HR Email

A separate onboarding email is sent to HR containing the information generated for the HR team.

This allows HR to receive the onboarding details without manually preparing the notification.

Setup
1. Import the Workflow

Import the n8n workflow JSON file into your n8n instance.

2. Configure Webhook

Configure your employee onboarding form or application to send an HTTP POST request to the n8n Webhook.

3. Configure Google Gemini

Add your Google Gemini credentials and connect them to the Google Gemini Chat Model node.

4. Configure Google Sheets

Connect your Google Sheets account and select the spreadsheet used for storing onboarding records.

5. Configure Gmail

Connect your Gmail account and configure the recipients used for employee and HR communications.

6. Test the Workflow

Submit employee onboarding information and verify that:

The webhook receives the data.
JavaScript extracts the employee information.
Google Gemini analyzes the data.
Missing onboarding information is identified.
Equipment requirements are processed.
Employee and HR emails are generated.
The onboarding record is stored in Google Sheets.
Emails are sent to the employee and HR.
